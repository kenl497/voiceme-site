VoiceMe Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

VoiceMe is built to protect privacy. The app does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet to operate.

What stays on your device

The things you type and speak, your saved phrases and boards, history, the activity log, and your settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app removes them.

Speech

VoiceMe uses the text-to-speech voices built into iOS. Speech is produced on your device.

Optional sign-in

You can sign in with Apple, but you don't have to. If you do, Apple may share your name and email address with the app, depending on your choice. They are saved only on your device, in the app's settings, and are never sent to us. Choose Delete Account in Settings to remove them. Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple and subject to Apple's own privacy policy.

Admin PIN

If you set a caregiver PIN, it is stored in your device's Keychain and is never sent anywhere.

Children

VoiceMe is designed with children in mind. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
