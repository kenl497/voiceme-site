Common questions

How do I change the voice, speed, or pitch?

Open Settings in the app. Under Voice and Speech you can pick a voice and adjust rate, pitch, and volume. Only voices already downloaded on your device appear. To get more voices, open iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

Which typing mode should I use?

Settings → Input Mode has three options: Simple, Paragraph Builder, and Free Text. You can change it any time. Start with Simple for a beginner who types one word at a time.

What does the space bar do?

With Space Bar Speaks on, pressing space speaks what was typed and clears the field. Turn it off in Settings if you want the space bar to act as a normal space.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Apple is optional, and everything works without it. To remove an account, open Settings and choose Delete Account.

How do I delete my data?

Everything stays on your device. In Settings, choose Clear All History & Phrases or Clear Everything. Deleting the app also removes its data.

The app isn't speaking

Check that your device isn't on silent and that the volume is up. Then try a different voice in Settings.
