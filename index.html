Type it, and VoiceMe says it.

VoiceMe is a simple, fast app that gives non-verbal people a voice. Type with the on-screen keyboard or an external one, and VoiceMe speaks every word out loud.

Three ways to type, to fit each person
  • Simple: one word or phrase at a time, with recent phrases for quick access.
  • Paragraph Builder: words collect into a paragraph you can undo, read back, or finish.
  • Free Text: write freely, and press Enter to speak and save what you typed.

Made with care

VoiceMe was inspired by our daughter and built with non-verbal children in mind. It needs no account, collects no data, and works without an internet connection.
